The cover prints on a separate sheet inside the printer margin. Cut at the trim marks to remove the white border; extra artwork (bleed) continues beyond each cut for ink to the finished edge. Increase the margin if your printer clips the artwork or marks. Keep text away from the cuts. The finished cover is smaller than the chosen paper; use larger paper if needed. Print at actual size (100%). A two-page cover supplies both sides; booklets always reserve a reverse side.
